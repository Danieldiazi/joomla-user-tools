# Joomla User Tools screenshots

[Español](capturas.md) · **English**

[Back to the README](../README.en.md)

The earlier screenshots show the Spanish interface on Joomla 6.2.0. Each caption gives the version the screenshot was taken with when it is recent: deletion in 2026.10.5, remove from groups in 2026.10.9 and 2026.10.16, the comparison review and groups and access levels in 2026.10.11, content by access level in 2026.10.12, access diagnosis and the content by age list in 2026.10.15, move groups in 2026.10.16, add accounts to groups, export accounts and undo blocks in 2026.10.19, the account profile in 2026.10.20, and the content by age table with its four kinds and the guided tour in the version after 2026.10.20. Screenshots without a version show an older interface and may differ from the current one. For current options and behaviour, see the [README](../README.en.md) and [FAQ](../FAQ.en.md).

The home screen, four categories and Users dashboard were captured on **31 October 2026** on the disposable Joomla 6.2.0 CI site, with the **English interface** and fictional data, using the changes in PR #57 after 2026.10.20. Both galleries share these images. They do not represent a new published release.

CI checks keyboard order, menu expansion and the active category. Images were inspected at 200% text size, with a dark preference and at 390 px width. The cards fit at 390 px, but Joomla’s overall header produces a document width of 600 px. The template has not been altered to hide this. This does not establish WCAG conformance or replace screen-reader testing.
